# Celebration on the „Hotovo" screen

## What we decided

- The „Hotovo" screen celebrates that the child finished. It is the moment when the child
  hands the exercise back to the parent. The guide girl says goodbye in the video, so this
  screen has **no mascot**.
- Natural and cheerful, not over the top: one short animation (about 3 s) that plays
  once, and **one big title**. No subtitle.
- **No numbers, no days, no collecting.** Nothing new is saved.
- **Built in code, not with Lottie** (decided 2026-11-05). The first try used two Lottie
  files generated by a script; the motion looked mechanical (confetti hung in a ring, the
  glow didn't follow the sun, a small dark icon). The celebrations are simple shapes, so
  React Native `Animated` and `react-native-svg` draw them exactly like the approved
  mockups, with colors from `constants/theme.js`. Lottie only makes sense later for
  animations drawn by a designer.
- **For now five celebrations**, picked at random each time:
  - **Sun** — pops in and hops twice, yellow rays and confetti („Hurá!")
  - **Star** — spins in like a coin and sways twice, apricot rays and confetti („Paráda!")
  - **Balloon** — flies up from below and sways, orange rays („Skvěle!")
  - **Rocket** — flies up from below and sways, teal rays („Super!")
  - **Paw** — rolls in from the right and sways, apricot rays („Bravo!")

## How it works

```
components/celebration/
  Celebration.jsx   dumb component: rays + confetti + the moving circle + the title
  Rays.jsx          soft rays (SVG slices with a fading gradient), appear and turn slowly
  Confetti.jsx      11 pieces burst from the center, then fall and fade out
  celebrations.js   the list: icon, motion, color names, title
app/done.jsx        smart screen: picks a celebration at random, turns color names into
                    palette values, saves today
```

Timing (one run, no loop):

| Time                  | What happens                                         |
| --------------------- | ---------------------------------------------------- |
| 0–0.6 s (up to 1.2 s) | the circle comes in with a small bounce              |
| from 0.3 s            | the rays fade in and turn 110° over 8 s              |
| 0.5–2.6 s             | the confetti bursts out, then falls 260 px and fades |
| after landing         | two hops (dance) or two gentle sways (the others)    |
| 1–1.5 s               | the title fades in and rises 14 px                   |
| 1.8–2.6 s             | the „Hotovo" button fades in (`done.jsx`)            |

- Every animation uses `useNativeDriver: true`, so it stays smooth.
- Colors: the `celebration…` colors in `constants/theme.js` (both palettes), plus
  `tertiary`, `primary` and `secondary`.
- The stage is only decoration, so screen readers skip it; the title is read as a header.

**The screen and the button** (`app/done.jsx`, agreed 2026-11-07):

- One column with flexible gaps, `<Spacer flex>` 4 : 1 : 2 (the middle gap at least
  24 px), and the bottom safe-area inset as padding. The gaps shrink and grow, so the
  look is the same on small and big phones; no large fixed paddings.
- „Hotovo" is a primary (blue) `ThemedButton`, 220 px wide, centred, in the lower third
  where the thumb reaches.
- It fades in after the title. Until then it can't be tapped (`pointerEvents`) and screen
  readers skip it, so a stray tap from the child doesn't close the celebration.
- It leads to „Kalendář" with `dismissTo`, so the celebration is closed and you can't go
  back to it.

**To add a celebration:** add one item to `celebrations.js` with a Lucide icon, a motion
(`'dance'`, `'spin'`, `'float'`, `'roll'`) and color names. A new kind of motion (wiggle,
bounce…) is a new transform list in `Celebration.jsx` plus a line in `TIMING`.

## Ideas for later (not decided)

- **More celebrations.** Ideas we sketched: rainbow, fish with bubbles, flower with
  hearts … with other motions (wiggle, bounce like a ball, jelly, swing) and effects (confetti rain, bubbles, sparkles, fireworks, falling leaves).
- **A short cheerful melody** with `expo-audio`, files in `components/celebration/sounds/`
  (free sounds: Pixabay, Freesound; CC0 is best). Later maybe a „Zvuky oslav" switch in
  Nastavení.
- **Reduce motion:** when the phone has „reduce motion" on
  (`AccessibilityInfo.isReduceMotionEnabled`), show the circle and title without moving.
- **A gentle vibration** with `expo-haptics`.
